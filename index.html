Frequently asked questions.

Is it accurate?

Prices pull live from StockX, GOAT, and eBay — the same markets you already trust. Real sold prices, not estimates.

Can't I just check StockX?

Sure — one item at a time. MyValue is your whole collection, live, in one place, with alerts and history.

Why pay?

Unlimited tracking, real-time alerts, and full analytics for less than a coffee a month.

How much does it cost?

Free to start, with no card required. MyValue Plus is $5.99/month for early members — that rate stays locked for as long as you stay subscribed, while new members pay $9.99/month once early access ends. Prefer to pay yearly? Plus is $49.99/year, which works out to two months free.

What can I track?

Sneakers, watches, cars, tech, and collectibles at launch — with more asset classes on the way.

Is my collection private?

Yes. Your portfolio is private by default and encrypted — nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. Our privacy policy sets out exactly what is encrypted and what we never share or sell.
