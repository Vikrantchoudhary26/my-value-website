1. Introduction

Panthi Enterprises, doing business as MyValue (“MyValue”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our web application located at https://myvalue-nine.vercel.app (and associated domains, including https://getmyvalue.app).

2. Information We Collect

We prioritize collecting market data over personal identifying information. We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you register or sign in via standard authentication providers (such as Google, Apple, or Firebase Auth), we collect your name, email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Item Photographs & Valuation Data: When you upload images of items (sneakers, watches, collectibles, tech, etc.) to assess their estimated market value, these images are processed solely to identify items and retrieve valuation metrics.
  • Market Data & Portfolio Activity: Information regarding items in your tracked collection, target price alerts (Buy/Sell Targets), and portfolio preferences.
  • Analytics & Technical Data: We use Google Analytics and cookie-based telemetry to collect standard log information, including IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction data to improve application performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the MyValue service.
  • Process uploaded item photos to identify assets and display live market values.
  • Manage user accounts and subscriptions.
  • Analyze site usage trends via Google Analytics to optimize app experience.
  • Send critical account and subscription notices.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted service providers to run our application:

  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Vercel
  • Database & Authentication: Firebase (Google)
  • Analytics: Google Analytics
  • Marketplace APIs: Market data standard providers (e.g., eBay, StockX, GOAT APIs) for live market price updates.

5. Data Security

Your data is encrypted in transit (using SSL/TLS) and at rest. We implement industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards through our cloud infrastructure providers (Vercel and Firebase).

6. Children’s Privacy

MyValue is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 18 without parental oversight as required by applicable Texas regulations). If you believe a minor has submitted personal data, please contact us at Support@getmyvalue.app for prompt removal.

7. Texas Privacy Rights

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), Texas residents have specific rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at Support@getmyvalue.app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
